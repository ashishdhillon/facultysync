How My Day works

  1. Collect. The WhatsApp bridge reads your watched chats; Gmail and Outlook are checked automatically. Nothing is ever sent.
  2. Filter. Greetings and chatter are skipped. Messages that mention you, have a deadline, a document or words like exam, marks or meeting become tasks.
  3. Act. Tick a task when done (you can undo), snooze it, or edit its title and date. Your edits always win over the AI.
  4. Plan. Each morning, or when you press Plan my day, My Day fits your deadlines around today's classes.

Press ? any time to see keyboard shortcuts.

Add your timetable

Copy timetable.example.json to data/timetable.json in the whatsapp-bridge folder and list your classes for each day (24-hour times, for example 09:30). My Day then shows today's classes and plans tasks around them. No restart needed.
