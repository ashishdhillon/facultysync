AI planner. Add an API key so My Day can write clear actions, find deadlines and plan your day. Your key is stored in the Mac Keychain. Only task titles, short snippets (max 300 characters), sender names, dates, your classes and project names are sent to the AI.

🗓️ Add your timetable

Copy timetable.example.json to data/timetable.json in the whatsapp-bridge folder and list your classes for each day (24-hour times, for example 09:30). My Day then shows today's classes and plans tasks around them. No restart needed.
